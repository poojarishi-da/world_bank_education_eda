# World Bank Global Education Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of the World Bank 
EdStats dataset — a comprehensive global education 
database covering 3,665 indicators across 214 countries 
from 1970 to 2017. This project was completed as part 
of the AlmaBetter Advanced Certification in Data Science 
& AI Engineering Capstone Project.

## 🎯 Business Objective
To uncover meaningful patterns in global literacy rates, 
gender disparities, regional differences, income group 
variations and education spending trends — providing 
data-driven insights for international development 
organizations, governments and NGOs to make informed 
education investment decisions.

## ❓ Key Questions Answered
1. Is there a gender disparity in adult literacy rates 
   across regions and income groups?
2. Does higher government education spending (% of GDP) 
   lead to higher literacy rates?
3. Which countries and regions invest most in education?
4. How do literacy rates differ across income groups?

## 📊 Dataset
- **Source:** World Bank EdStats
- **Files:** EdStatsData.csv, EdStatsCountry.csv, 
  EdStatsSeries.csv, EdStatsFootNote.csv
- **Size:** 886,930 rows × 70 columns (main file)
- **Indicators used:** Adult literacy rate 
  (both sexes, female, male) and Government 
  expenditure on education as % of GDP

## 🔍 Key Findings
1. Education spending has near-zero correlation (0.12) 
   with literacy rates — spending more does not 
   guarantee better outcomes
2. Low income countries have a 19.61 percentage point 
   gender literacy gap (female 42% vs male 62%)
3. Europe & Central Asia achieves 98.76% literacy 
   with only 3.80% GDP spending — efficiency matters 
   more than budget size
4. Sub-Saharan Africa has a standard deviation of 
   22.27% in literacy rates — requires country-specific 
   strategies not regional ones
